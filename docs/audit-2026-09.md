# Platform audit — September 2026

## Baseline

The stable `main` branch is a coherent, production-oriented MVP. At audit time it passed ESLint, strict TypeScript, 9 unit tests, and the Next.js production build. Its strongest foundations are cryptographically random public tag IDs, hashed activation credentials, atomic tag claiming, owner-scoped tag access, server-authoritative pricing, Stripe signature verification, and privacy-minimised scan records.

## Gaps that block commercial launch

- Checkout requires an account and the order model cannot represent a guest.
- Product administration is absent; the only admin workflow is NFC credential generation.
- Product categories, media, configurable options, inventory reservations, fulfilment history, and customer administration are absent.
- Email/password sessions are custom and social authentication/account linking are not implemented.
- Stripe completion does not yet reconcile the paid amount, currency, and Checkout Session against the stored payment before transitioning the order.
- The development admin seed was not blocked in production and local setup omitted required Docker variables and the host database port.
- Forwarded client IP headers were trusted without an explicit trusted-proxy deployment setting.
- Public tag analytics are written in the request lifecycle and need an eventual queue/aggregation path at scale.
- The CSP includes development allowances, media upload is not implemented, and legal pages remain placeholders.

## Evolution approach

Keep a modular monolith while the business is early. Separate catalogue, checkout, identity, NFC, manufacturing, and administration behind domain services and validated route boundaries. Keep PostgreSQL/Prisma as the source of truth, introduce immutable order-item snapshots, use an object-storage-compatible media boundary, and split services only when operational scale justifies it.

All commercial development is performed on `develop`. `main` remains the stable MVP until an explicit, evidence-backed release approval.
